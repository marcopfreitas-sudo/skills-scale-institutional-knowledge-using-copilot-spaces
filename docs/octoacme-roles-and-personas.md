# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Technical Lead / Architecture Lead

### Role Summary
Technical Leads provide technical strategy and architecture guidance to ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with organizational standards. They collaborate with developers and project leadership to balance technical excellence with delivery timelines.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical architecture and design patterns for projects
- Review technical designs and code quality standards
- Identify and mitigate technical risks and dependencies
- Mentor developers and drive technical capability growth
- Ensure alignment with organizational technology standards
- Participate in capacity planning and effort estimation
- Lead technical design reviews and spike investigations

### Goals
- Reduce technical debt and improve system maintainability
- Enable team technical capability growth
- Balance innovation with stability
- Reduce production incidents through sound architecture

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Guide implementation, review designs, provide mentorship
- **With Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **With Project Managers**: Identify technical risks, estimate effort, manage dependencies
- **With Security Officer**: Ensure security best practices in architecture
- **With QA Lead**: Collaborate on test automation strategy and approach

### Success Metrics
- Technical debt ratio and reduction over time
- Code review quality and time-to-review
- Architecture decisions documented and followed
- Team technical capability growth
- Production incident reduction rate

### Typical Communication
- Architecture design reviews and spike sessions
- Technical risk assessments
- Mentoring sessions with developers
- Code review comments and guidance

---

## QA Lead / Quality Assurance Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy, testing approach, and acceptance criteria validation. They ensure that deliverables meet quality standards and customer expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality standards and testing strategy
- Create and maintain test plans and acceptance criteria
- Lead testing activities (manual, automated, integration, e2e)
- Identify and track quality issues and regressions
- Collaborate on Definition of Done
- Mentor QA team members and drive test automation
- Establish quality metrics and dashboards

### Goals
- Ensure consistent product quality across releases
- Reduce defect escape rate and critical issues
- Improve test coverage and automation ratio
- Enable rapid and confident releases

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Define acceptance criteria, validate implementation, provide rapid feedback
- **With Product Managers**: Clarify requirements, validate feature completeness
- **With Project Managers**: Report quality status, manage testing timeline
- **With Technical Lead**: Align on test automation strategy and approach
- **With Security Officer**: Include security test scenarios and compliance checks

### Success Metrics
- Defect escape rate and severity tracking
- Test coverage and automation ratio
- Time-to-fix for critical issues
- Customer-reported defects post-release
- Testing cycle time reduction

### Typical Communication
- Quality status reports in standups and syncs
- Test plan reviews and acceptance criteria discussions
- Defect triage and tracking
- Testing ceremony facilitation

---

## Stakeholder / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Stakeholders represent business interests and decision-making authority. They provide strategic context, resource approval, and business alignment for projects.

### Responsibilities
- Define business objectives and success metrics
- Approve project charter and resource allocation
- Make business trade-off decisions
- Provide executive alignment and remove organizational blockers
- Review progress and major decisions
- Communicate outcomes to leadership and external parties

### Goals
- Ensure business value realization
- Secure necessary resources and executive support
- Remove organizational barriers to execution
- Drive alignment across dependent teams

### Key Interactions
- **With Project Managers**: Receive status updates, make approval decisions
- **With Product Managers**: Align on roadmap and priorities
- **With Delivery Team**: Understand risks and decisions
- **With Security Officer**: Approve security and compliance requirements

### Success Metrics
- Project delivery against committed timelines
- Business impact realization
- Stakeholder satisfaction and alignment
- Resource efficiency and utilization

### Typical Communication
- Weekly or bi-weekly status updates
- Executive steering committee meetings
- Decision approval and escalation resolution
- Outcome communication and announcements

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate team processes, remove blockers, and drive continuous improvement. They enable teams to self-organize and deliver value iteratively.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate ceremonies (standups, planning, retrospectives, reviews)
- Remove impediments and unblock the team
- Coach team on Agile practices and continuous improvement
- Track metrics and burndown
- Facilitate retrospectives and action item tracking
- Help resolve team conflicts and interpersonal issues
- Promote psychological safety and team health

### Goals
- Improve team velocity and predictability
- Reduce cycle time and deployment frequency
- Build high-performing, self-organizing teams
- Drive continuous process improvement

### Key Interactions
- **With All Team Members**: Support adoption of Agile practices
- **With Project Managers**: Coordinate planning and risk management
- **With Leadership**: Escalate blockers, report team health
- **With Product Managers**: Support backlog refinement and grooming

### Success Metrics
- Team velocity and consistency
- Cycle time reduction
- Action item completion rate
- Team retrospective engagement and sentiment
- On-time delivery rate

### Typical Communication
- Sprint ceremony facilitation
- Action item tracking and follow-up
- One-on-one coaching sessions
- Team health and engagement surveys

---

## Security Officer / Security Champion

### Role Summary
Security Officers ensure that projects meet security compliance requirements and threat assessments. They integrate security into the development lifecycle and drive security awareness.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct threat assessments and security reviews
- Define security requirements and acceptance criteria
- Perform security testing and code review
- Ensure compliance with security standards and regulations
- Participate in incident response and forensics
- Drive security awareness and best practices training
- Maintain security risk register and mitigation status

### Goals
- Prevent security breaches and data loss
- Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements
- Build security-conscious development culture
- Reduce time-to-remediation for vulnerabilities

### Key Interactions
- **With Developers**: Review code for vulnerabilities, provide guidance
- **With Technical Lead**: Advise on secure architecture and design patterns
- **With QA Lead**: Include security test scenarios and penetration testing
- **With Product Managers**: Define security requirements and user privacy needs
- **With Project Managers**: Track security risks and compliance status

### Success Metrics
- Vulnerability detection and remediation time
- Security compliance audit results
- Security training completion rate
- Incident frequency and time-to-resolution
- Security risk trend and mitigation progress

### Typical Communication
- Security requirement reviews and threat modeling sessions
- Code review comments on security topics
- Compliance status reports
- Incident response and post-mortem facilitation

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation. They gather requirements, document user stories, and validate that solutions meet business objectives.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business requirements
- Create detailed user stories with acceptance criteria
- Validate solutions against business requirements
- Facilitate workshops and stakeholder discussions
- Maintain requirements traceability
- Support UAT and change management
- Identify and escalate scope changes and impacts

### Goals
- Ensure alignment between business needs and delivery
- Reduce rework and scope creep
- Improve requirements clarity and traceability
- Accelerate stakeholder acceptance and adoption

### Key Interactions
- **With Stakeholders**: Gather requirements and validate scope
- **With Product Managers**: Define features, user stories, and prioritization
- **With Developers**: Clarify requirements, support implementation questions
- **With QA Lead**: Define and validate acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers**: Manage scope and change requests

### Success Metrics
- Requirements clarity and completeness
- Rework due to requirement ambiguity
- Stakeholder satisfaction with requirements
- UAT defect rate and resolution time
- Scope change frequency and impact

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering and refinement sessions
- User story creation and review
- UAT support and coordination
- Scope impact analysis and communication

- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance
- Refer to key interactions to understand cross-functional collaboration requirements
- Use success metrics to define role-specific success criteria and OKRs
